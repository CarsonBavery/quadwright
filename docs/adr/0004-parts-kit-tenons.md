# ADR-0004: Parts-based kit with integral tenons

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-02

## Context
Buildings are milled separately and assembled without glue, and must not rotate or be placed wrongly.

## Decision
Each part gets an integral footprint-shaped tenon matching a pocket in the base; small parts fall back to two dowels.

## Consequences
Self-registering, glue-free assembly. Rejected: a single monolithic mesh.
